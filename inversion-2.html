    Input: formal and narrative inversions

    These five structures are more about sequence of events and formal tone than about a direct argument. They add range, but only when they fit. Each one has a “when NOT to use it” note.

    Why it matters

    In Part 1 you learned safe, high-impact inversions. These five are different: precision, not quantity. Use one or two in an essay, in the right place.

    Band 6The scandal was so damaging.
    →
    Band 7+Such was the scandal that …

    Reference table: five structures

    the trigger and the inverted verbthe rest of the sentence

    The same rule, bigger triggers

    trigger phrase + AUXILIARY + SUBJECT + main verb

    In Part 1 the trigger was often one word (Never, Rarely). Here it is often a whole phrase: Hardly … when, Not until …, Only after …. The auxiliary still moves in front of the subject. The main verb stays.

    Not thisNot until regulations forced them to change many companies took climate change seriously.
    →
    ThisNot until regulations forced them to change did many companies take climate change seriously.

    Only the main clause inverts. The clause after Not until / Only after keeps normal order.

        Text 1 · Opinion · climate policy

        Climate policy has changed slowly over the last thirty years. ANot until regulations forced them to change did many companies take climate change seriously. BHardly had new rules been introduced when critics warned about their cost. CSuch was the pressure from voters that governments agreed to stricter targets. DOnly after targets became legally binding did emissions begin to fall. For these reasons, clear and binding rules are essential.
