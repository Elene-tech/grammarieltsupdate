    8–22 min

    Input: one rule for all five structures

    Inversion means putting the auxiliary verb before the subject, after a “trigger” phrase at the start of the sentence. It adds emphasis and shows grammatical range.

    Why it matters

    Band 6 sentences “work”. Band 7+ sentences show that the writer controls the sentence. Inversion is one of the fastest ways to show it. Today: five structures that are safe and high-impact.

    Band 6Technology has changed communication.
    →
    Band 7+Not only has technology changed communication, …

    Reference table: five structures

    the trigger and the inverted verbthe rest of the sentence

    The mechanics: only the auxiliary moves

    trigger phrase + AUXILIARY + SUBJECT + main verb

    Not thisNot only technology has changed communication, …
    →
    ThisNot only has technology changed communication, …

    The main verb stays in its place. Only the auxiliary (has, does, did, can, is …) moves in front of the subject. If the sentence has no auxiliary, add do / does / did: Not only does social media affect teenagers.
