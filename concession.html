    Input: admit one point, argue another

    A concession admits the opposite point, then returns to your own argument. Five structures do this job.

    Why it matters

    Grammatical Range and Accuracy is one of the four Writing criteria. Concession is one of the fastest ways to show range.

    Band 6I agree or I disagree. No shades.
    →
    Band 7+I admit the opposite point and argue back.

    The “this” test

    After despite and in spite of you need a noun phrase, not a clause. Put this in its place: Despite this works, Despite he works does not.

    Not thisDespite technology has changed communication, …
    →
    ThisDespite the profound changes brought by technology, …

    An -ing form also works: Despite working long hours, … “Despite the fact that + clause” is correct, but it is long. A noun phrase is shorter and sounds more academic.

        Text 1 · Discuss both views · free university education

        Many people argue that university education should be free. ADespite the high cost to taxpayers, free tuition brings clear benefits for society. BAlthough graduates earn more on average, many talented young people cannot afford to start a degree. CWhile student loans help some families, the debt can follow graduates for decades. DAdmittedly, fully free education would be expensive. Nevertheless, the long-term gains in skills and fairness would justify the investment. For these reasons, governments should reduce or remove tuition fees.

BEven though shop owners fear losing customers, evidence from several cities suggests that pedestrian areas often increase sales. CWhilst people with disabilities must still be able to reach the centre, special permits can solve this problem. Overall, the advantages clearly outweigh the disadvantages.
