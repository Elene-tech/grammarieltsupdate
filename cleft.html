Choose the correct sentence

    8–22 min

    Input: put the key idea in the spotlight

    A cleft sentence splits one idea into two parts. The important information goes into a “spotlight” position. This adds emphasis and variety to your essay.

    Why it matters

    Band 6 sentences are all built the same way: subject, verb, object. A cleft sentence lets you choose what the reader notices first or last.

    Band 6Education gives young people the best chance of a stable career.
    →
    Band 7+It is education that gives young people the best chance of a stable career.

    Reference table: six cleft structures

    the framethe spotlight (focus)

    Check the grammar of each part

    A cleft sentence has two parts: a frame and a focus. The focus is the idea you want to emphasise. Ask: what is the most important idea?

    Normal sentenceGovernments should invest more in prevention.
    →
    Cleft sentenceWhat governments should do is invest more in prevention.

    Do not use clefts in every sentence. One or two per essay are enough. Use them for your main idea, your solution, or when you correct a common belief.
